 .--------------------------------------------------------------------------.
 |      WHY LAMERS RUIN THE SCENE  --  an editorial by Carrier Lost         |
 `--------------------------------------------------------------------------'

  I have been calling boards for almost three years. I have seen a LOT.
  and i am tired. so here is my honest list of what makes a lamer, and
  why they are making the scene worse for the rest of us.

  1. THE "PLEEEEASE" GUY
     Posts "can anyone upload X" 14 times in 14 bases. does not read the
     file area. does not read the bulletins. does not read ANYTHING.

  2. THE FAKE
     Uploads a game from last year, renames it, writes a fake NFO and puts
     a cool group name on it. the group never heard of him. the sysop has
     to clean it up. you know who you are.

  3. THE TYPOGRAPHER
     tYpEs LiKe ThIs aNd tHiNkS iT mAkEs HiM eLiTe. it doesnt. it makes
     your messages hard to read. also please stop typing in CAPS lock.

  4. THE SPAMMER
     Posts the same board ad everywhere. in every echo. in every base.
     in the Sysop Feedback base. yes, that one too.

  5. THE LEECH
     takes, takes, takes. never gives. gets deleted. comes back with a new
     handle. gets deleted. comes back. you get it.

  THE PROBLEM
  -----------
    A lamer doesnt know hes a lamer. thats the whole thing. he thinks hes
    helping, or hes playing a game. meanwhile the sysop spends his weekend
    cleaning the file base.

  THE FIX
  -------
    Be nice. Teach the new guys. Read the rules BEFORE you post. If someone
    asks a dumb question once, answer it. If they ask it again, point to
    the FAQ. If they ask it a third time, ok, roll your eyes. quietly.

    Everybody was new once. Even me. Especially me. I once asked what a
    "ratio" was in the middle of a chat session. oh the shame.

  Nobody is born elite. but you can be a decent person from your first call.

                                              Carrier Lost, 06-93
                                              greets: all the nice lamers
