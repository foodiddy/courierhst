 ==========================================================================
   AN OPEN LETTER TO THE LEECHES  --  from the sysop of The Silent Archive
 ==========================================================================

  To whom it may concern (you know who you are):

  It is 3 AM. I am typing this on a 386 that makes a noise like a lawnmower,
  looking at my user log. 214 callers this month. 41 uploads. you do the
  math. i did, and it made me sad.

  Running a board is not free. let me tell you what it costs me:

    * a second phone line:                     about $20 a month
    * electricity for a computer that never sleeps
    * hard drive space. 1.2 gigs cost me more than my first car stereo.
    * time. oh god, the TIME.

  I do it because I love it. i love the weird little community, the guy
  in Duluth who sends ANSi art, the girl who runs the trivia contest.
  but the guy who logs on, downloads 20 files, and logs off without a word?
  he is not part of that. he is a vending machine user.

  WHAT I ASK:
    1. Upload something once in a while. Even a small utility.
    2. Say hi in the message bases. Say thanks. It costs nothing.
    3. If you find a bug, tell me. Dont just complain to your friends.
    4. If you get your ratio bumped, pass it on to the next guy.

  WHAT I WILL DO:
    - Keep the board free. No pay-to-play. Ever.
    - Validate uploads within 48 hours. I promise.
    - Keep the bad files out so the good stuff is easy to find.

  IF YOU WONT PLAY BALL:
    The delete key is right next to the enter key, and i know how to
    use both. I dont want to. Please dont make me.

  Thanks to the 40 or so of you who DO give back. You keep this thing alive.
  You know who you are too.

                                       Yours in 14.4k,
                                       Count Zer0
                                       Sysop, The Silent Archive (555-0142)
                                       "Upload a file. Make my day."
