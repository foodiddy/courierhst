  This is not THE manifesto. There are enough of those. This is a small one,
  written on the back of a phone bill, for anyone who needs reminding.

  1.  LEARN HOW THINGS WORK.
      Take the lid off. If you can't take the lid off, read the manual. If
      there is no manual, write one when you are done.

  2.  SHARE WHAT YOU FIND.
      A secret is a bug in a person. Pass the knowledge on.

  3.  DO NOT BREAK WHAT YOU DO NOT OWN.
      Looking is fine. Learning is better. Wrecking someone's work is the
      mark of a lamer. If you crash it, you owe the owner an apology.

  4.  DO NOT STEAL FROM THE LITTLE GUY.
      The sysop who runs a board out of a spare bedroom pays the phone bill
      from a paycheck. Be kind to them.

  5.  JUDGE PEOPLE BY WHAT THEY KNOW AND DO.
      Not by age, grades, haircut, size of their hard drive or how they
      look in the school photo. Some of the best coders I know are fourteen.
      Some are fifty-four.

  6.  CURIOSITY IS NOT A CRIME.
      It sometimes looks like one to people who haven't met it before.

  7.  GIVE CREDIT.
      Write your name on your own work. Never write it on someone else's.

  8.  BE EXCELLENT.
      Real skill is rare enough that you will recognize it at a glance.

  9.  KNOW WHEN TO STOP.
      The coolest thing a hacker can do is walk away from a thing that is
      going to hurt someone, and leave a note explaining why.

  10. TAKE OUT THE TRASH.
      Close the doors you opened. Put the cables back. Log off cleanly.
