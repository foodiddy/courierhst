  CHAPTER 3:  THE ULTIMATE PRANK

  Changing every clock in the house by 5 minutes.

  Materials: one house, a free evening, a sense of mischief.
  Procedure:

    1. Wait until everyone is out. A dentist appointment works well.
    2. Walk from room to room and move each clock by five minutes.
    3. Do the oven clock. Do the VCR. Do the one on the microwave
       that never worked anyway.
    4. Sit back. For a week, every person in the house will be five minutes
       late, five minutes early or deeply confused.
    5. Do not confess. At least not until Sunday.

  RESULT: nobody is hurt, nobody can prove anything, and your little
  brother is still convinced it's his fault. Pure art.

  CHAPTER 4:  BUILDING A PILLOW FORT OF UNUSUAL SIZE

  The key is a good foundation. Start with the couch cushions, add every
  blanket in the house, and finish with the dining room chairs. Check the
  ceiling height. Never build over the cat.

    (sysop note: it was never going to be there. This is a joke.)

  ...so instead, here is a recipe you CAN follow:

    NOT-SO-DANGEROUS BANANA BREAD
      three ripe bananas, mashed with a fork
      one cup of sugar and a pinch of salt
      two cups of flour, a spoon of baking powder
      butter, an egg or two, and a little patience
    Mix it all, put it in a tin, and bake it in a warm oven until your
    house smells wonderful. Eat it before your sister does.

  CHAPTER 6:  MAKING FRIENDS WITH YOUR PARENTS' ANSWERING MACHINE

  Leave a polite message. Say your name and the time. Do NOT hum into it.
  Do NOT leave a 14-minute message about your day. It records all of it.

  CHAPTER 7:  HOW TO LOOK BUSY WHILE WAITING FOR A DOWNLOAD

  1. Tape a sign to your monitor that says "Transfer in progress".
  2. Hold a book. Turn the page every few minutes.
  3. Wait for the "ding" at the end. Pretend it is a surprise.
