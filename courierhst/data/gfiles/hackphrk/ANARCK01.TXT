  CHAPTER 1:  HOW TO ANNOY YOUR SYSOP

  Step 1.  Log on. Say HELLO. Say it again, in capital letters.
  Step 2.  Page the sysop. When he answers, say "just testing".
  Step 3.  Ask where the files are. They are in the file area, which is
           reached by pressing F.
  Step 4.  Ask again.
  Step 5.  Upload an empty file. Name it README.
  Step 6.  Hang up mid-sentence and call back with a new handle.

  Congratulations, you are now a lamer. Please see CHAPTER 5 of "How to
  not be a lamer", available on better boards.

  ---- end of chapter 1 -------------------------------------------------

  CHAPTER 2:  MAKING THE PERFECT 3AM POT OF COFFEE

  You need: water, coffee, a filter, and the will to continue.

  1. Fill the pot with water. Cold, from the tap.
  2. Put the filter in the basket. Make sure it is the right way up.
  3. Spoon in coffee. Use more than you think. Then use some more.
  4. Press the switch. Watch the little light. Wait.
  5. Pour. Drink. Feel your eyelids stop weighing a kilogram each.
  6. Repeat until the download completes.

  TIP: If the pot is empty and so is the cupboard, you have found the real
  danger of this book. Go to bed.
