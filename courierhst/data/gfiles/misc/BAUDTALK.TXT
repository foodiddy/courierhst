                    A FIELD GUIDE TO MODEM SPEED
                    ----------------------------

  300 baud    "The tortoise." You can read faster than the text appears.
              Seen only in museums and in your uncle's stories.

  1200 baud   "The jogger." Getting there, slowly, with a lot of heavy
              breathing. Good for plain text and patience.

  2400 baud   "The commuter." Everyone has one. Nobody loves it. Downloads
              a small game in the time it takes to eat lunch.

  9600 baud   "The sports car." Costs a lot. Looks good in the driveway.
              Needs a good phone line, or it falls back to 2400 in shame.

  14400 baud  "The rocket." The speed you want. The speed your mom does
              not understand. Fast enough that you notice the line noise.

  16800 baud  "The rumour." HST Dual Standard from USR. Fast one way, and
              ok the other way. Only a few boards have it.

  28800 baud  "The legend." People say it exists. People say it will be
              here next year. Nobody has seen one at a copy party yet.

  WHAT THE LIGHTS MEAN
  --------------------
    HS ... High speed. The modem is happy.
    AA ... Auto answer. Waiting for a call.
    CD ... Carrier detect. Someone is out there.
    OH ... Off hook. The line is in use. Do not pick up the phone!
    RD/SD  Receive data and send data. Twinkle, twinkle, little light.

  THE THREE LAWS OF MODEM LIFE
  ----------------------------
    1. No matter how fast your modem, the other end is always slower.
    2. The line will drop at 99% of a download. Always. It is a law.
    3. Call waiting is the enemy. Ask the phone company how to switch it
       off, and tell your family not to pick up. They will anyway.
