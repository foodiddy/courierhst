             TOP 10 SIGNS YOU ARE A MODEM JUNKIE
             ===================================
        (as read at the monthly user meeting, 1993)

  10. Your mom answers the phone and you flinch because the line was
      supposed to be tied up till 2 AM.

   9. You can tell a 2400 from a 9600 just by the sound of the connect.

   8. The phone company knows you by first name. They stopped asking if
      you are "calling from a business".

   7. You have a drawer with 14 modem manuals. You have read them. All.

   6. You dream in ANSi. When you close your eyes you see a blue border
      and a cursor blinking in the corner.

   5. You describe your day as "two hours and fourteen minutes of uptime".

   4. You have said "I just need five more minutes" and meant it.
      Then you said it again an hour later, and meant it again.

   3. Your friends say "let's go see a movie" and you reply with the
      phone number of a board that has movie reviews.

   2. You know the busy signal so well that you can tell which board is
      busy by the pitch. "That's a Renegade board. Node 1. Always node 1."

   1. You are reading this file at 3 AM on a school night. Go to bed.

  BONUS: you laughed at at least four of these. Welcome to the club.
