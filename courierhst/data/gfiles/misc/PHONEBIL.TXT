  A TOTALLY SERIOUS MANUAL BY THE HOUSEHOLD DEFENSE LEAGUE

  This guide has no real methods in it. Not one. It only has comedy and
  advice that is mostly "be honest and sell your stuff". Read on.

  CHAPTER 1: THE BILL ARRIVES
  ---------------------------
    The bill arrives on a Tuesday. It is thick. It has a second page.
    You know the second page is yours. Fold your hands. Practice a calm
    face in the mirror. Do NOT say "it must be a mistake".

  CHAPTER 2: THE CONVERSATION
  ---------------------------
    Your parent will say your full name. This is how you know it is bad.
    They will hold up the bill, and point to the line that says "LONG
    DISTANCE: 14 hours 22 minutes". You will say something like:
    "That's... a lot of hours, isn't it?" This will not help.

  CHAPTER 3: HOW TO RESPOND
  -------------------------
    Option A: Offer to pay. With a job. Mowing lawns is a time-honored way
              to fund a hobby. Be ready to push a mower in July.
    Option B: Offer a trade: you give up the board for a month.
              (you will not survive. but you can try.)
    Option C: Explain that you are "learning computers". This works only
              if you can show a spreadsheet and a certificate.

  CHAPTER 4: THE FUTURE
  ---------------------
    Call LOCAL boards. They are free, and your neighbours are not scary.
    Find a board that is 5 miles from home. Befriend the sysop. Meet at a
    copy party someday and sit on a couch like a normal person.

  A FEW WORDS OF WISDOM
  ---------------------
    - Long distance is not free just because the modem is fast.
    - "It was only for a minute" is not a defence.
    - The smartest thing you can do is call a local board. Always.
    - The second smartest is hide the bill. (Just kidding. Do NOT do this.)

  Printed by the Household Defense League. Not a real organisation. The
  phone company is real, though, and it WILL send you the bill.
