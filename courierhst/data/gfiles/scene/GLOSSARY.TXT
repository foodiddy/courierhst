        THE COURIER'S GLOSSARY  --  WORDS YOU WILL HEAR ON THE BOARDS
        ============================================================

  Brand new here? Here is what everybody is talking about, in plain English.

  0-DAY ........ A release that is less than a day old. Also what the lamers
                 claim about stuff that is really three weeks old.
  ANSi ......... Colourful text art. Made with a drawing program, viewed with
                 a driver. The reason your screen looks like a rainbow.
  BAUD ......... How fast your modem talks. 2400 is slow. 14400 is elite.
                 28800 is a rumour.
  BUSY ......... What you hear when a good board has one node. Redial. Again.
  COURIER ...... Someone who carries releases from board to board. Needs
                 patience, a fast modem, and parents who dont check the phone.
  DISTRO SITE .. A board that receives releases early and passes them on.
  DIZ .......... Short for FILE_ID.DIZ, the description inside an archive.
  ELITE ........ Cool. Also spelled 3L1T3 by people who try too hard.
  FLAME ........ A rude public message. Dont write them, dont answer them.
  GREETS ....... The list of groups you wave at in the NFO. Be polite.
  HST .......... High Speed Technology, a USR modem protocol. Fast one way.
  LAMER ........ Somebody who takes and never gives. Or who types in ALL CAPS.
  LEECH ........ Downloads everything, uploads nothing. Ratios exist for them.
  NFO .......... The info file that comes with a release. Credits and notes.
  NODE ......... One phone line into a board. More nodes, fewer busy signals.
  NUKE ......... A release marked bad (broken, fake, or old). Gets deleted.
  RATIO ........ How much you must upload compared to what you download.
  SUPPLIER ..... The person who gets the original, first. Everything starts
                 with them.
  VALIDATE ..... When the sysop checks your upload and gives you credit.
  WAREZ ........ Software that gets passed around. Spelled with a Z because
                 that is how the cool kids spell it.
  ZMODEM ....... The file transfer protocol with crash recovery. Use it.

  Missing a word? Leave a note to Sector 7 in the Courier Lounge base and
  it will go in the next edition.
